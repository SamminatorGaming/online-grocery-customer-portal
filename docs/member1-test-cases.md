# Member 1 test cases

## Registration

A customer enters a name, valid email, and password. The system creates the account.

A customer tries to register with an email already used by another account. The system shows an error.

## Login

A customer enters the correct email and password. The system logs the customer in.

A customer enters the wrong password. The system does not log the customer in.

## Address

A logged-in customer enters a street, city, state, and zip code. The system saves the address.

A logged-in customer submits the address form without a zip code. The system shows an error.
